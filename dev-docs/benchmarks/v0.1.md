## Memory (R3 and the read contract)

Gates count Rust allocations with the driver's counting allocator, the unit of the bounds in `ARCHITECTURE.md`. Reads: the peak of the measured phase above a baseline taken after the open and after the destination is allocated, at most 3 x threads x chunk + 128 KiB of fixed bookkeeping. Writes: one member written in `max` through `PackBuilder` by `encode-memory`, at most 6 x threads x chunk + the in-memory index of that write + 256 KiB. Chunk is the largest `chunk_size` of the container. Peak RSS, which also holds code, zstd's C contexts and allocator retention, is reported and not gated, the pack's included.

## Reading these results (2026-11-06)

Run: a reduced set of 570 files (247 GB) of a private model library, near-duplicate models dropped
except the R6 finetune and the SDXL F32/fp16 unet pair; nice 19, ionice idle. Snapshot, pack, verify-exact and savings ran 2026-11-05 17:40 to 22:04;
source roots unchanged, 570 of 570 restored files bit-exact by an external `sha256sum`.

`load` and `random` were rerun 2026-11-06 19:26-20:32 (all runs `quiet`), and `encode-memory` added,
after two bench defects found in the first run were fixed:

- The first `random` run was hit by a RAID `check` of the array holding the pack (started by
  `mdcheck_continue.timer` at 00:41, 31 s into the packed repeat-1048576 run): packed warm p50 was
  161-184 ms and the step took 3 h. The guard now waits until every md `sync_action` is idle and marks
  runs with foreign disk traffic `disturbed`.
- The memory gates compared whole-process RSS with bounds stated in Rust allocations, and counted the
  index at its raw size while the in-memory index is about twice that. They now use the driver's
  counting allocator; RSS is reported only.

**Class gates.** The class targets were estimated on the full model set. The reduced set dropped
exactly the duplicates those estimates counted on, so the three `FAIL`s are not codec regressions:

- U8 packed: the whole 47.1% estimate was 16.6 GB of duplicates; none remain (dedup 0.01 GB).
- Q8_0: 2.48 GB of estimated duplicates are gone; entropy savings alone are 6.1% measured against
  5.5% estimated.
- F16: 7.2 of the estimated 23.5 GB remain, mostly SDXL, which measures 16-18% on its own (also on a
  separate SDXL F16 file outside the run).

Classes untouched by the selection (BF16, F32, FP8, K-quants) land within 1.3 pp of the estimate. Every
other gate passes. The tightest margin is Q8_0 read memory, 34.6 of 37.9 MB.
